Privacy Policy

Scioperi App — Last updated: 31 July 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the Scioperi app ("the App"), developed by Aport Solutions Ltd ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Information we collect

The App is designed to collect as little personal information as possible:

  • Anonymous device identifier. On first launch, the App generates a random identifier stored locally on your device. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple ID, and is used only to associate the alerts you create with your device.
  • Push notification token. If you grant permission, Apple assigns your device a push notification token, which we store so we can deliver strike alerts you have requested. You can disable this at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Alert preferences. Date ranges, transport sectors, and Italian regions/provinces you choose when creating an alert are stored on our servers, associated only with the anonymous identifier above.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require account creation, login, name, or email address.
  • We do not collect precise device location (region/province is selected manually by you, not detected automatically).
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, or advertising trackers.

How we use information

Information collected is used solely to operate the App's core functionality: showing strike information and sending you push notifications for alerts you have created. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Alert data and device tokens are retained until you delete the alert within the App, or until the App is uninstalled and the associated data is manually removed upon request.

Third-party services

The App displays publicly available strike data sourced from the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT). Push notifications are delivered via Apple's Push Notification service (APNs), subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below, providing the anonymous identifier if known, or simply by deleting your alerts within the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@aportsolutions.co.uk.
