Frequently asked questions and assistance for the Scioperi app

How do alerts work?

In the "My Alerts" section you can create an alert by specifying a date range, one or more transport sectors, and optionally a region or province. The app immediately shows any matching strikes and sends you a push notification if new matches appear later.

Where does the strike data come from?

Data comes directly from the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT) website, and is refreshed automatically several times a day.

I'm not receiving push notifications

Check that notifications are enabled for the app in iOS Settings → Notifications → Scioperi. Also make sure you have at least one active alert matching an upcoming strike.

Does the app require my location or personal data?

No. Scioperi does not require registration and does not collect personally identifiable data. Region/province are selected manually. For full details, see our privacy policy.
